Privacy policy

Updated 22 October 2026

This policy covers the IndoFreq vocabulary app for Android and the web, and this landing website. IndoFreq is an independent project maintained by mfarhancode.

What to know

Your detailed learning records stay primarily on your device. An account is optional. A cloud progress summary is not a complete backup of your learning data.

Learning data on your device

IndoFreq uses a local SQLite database for vocabulary and your learning records. These include flashcard and quiz activity, review schedules, Saved Words, Known Words, progress, streaks and your daily goal. Your optional profile name is saved locally. The app also remembers guest mode and local settings. Android reminder preferences are stored on the device.

In the web app, this data lives in browser storage for the app’s origin. Clearing that storage, using a temporary browsing session, or uninstalling or resetting an app can remove local data. Export a backup before changing devices or clearing storage.

Guest mode and accounts

You can learn as a guest without creating an account. Guest learning summaries are not uploaded to Firestore. The web app still needs an initial online visit to download its files.

Accounts use Firebase Authentication. Email/password registration supplies your email and password to Firebase. Google Sign-In uses Google and Firebase to authenticate you. Those services process account identifiers, authentication information and the technical information needed to provide and secure sign-in. The app does not include your password or authentication tokens in exported learning backups.

Progress summaries in Firestore

For signed-in users, IndoFreq can upload a summary associated with the Firebase account identifier. This happens through the profile’s upload action and the app’s existing background synchronization behavior. The summary contains:

  • Total words learned
  • Current streak
  • Last active date
  • Installed vocabulary version

This is an aggregate summary. It does not contain your full saved-word lists, per-word review schedules, detailed quiz history, or locally entered profile name. Signing in on another device does not restore those detailed records from the summary.

Exported backups

The app lets you export and import a JSON backup containing detailed learning data, including progress, word lists, learning history, daily goal and the local profile name. Exported files are saved to a location you choose; importing reads the file you select. The app’s export feature does not automatically upload the file to Firebase.

Keep backups somewhere you trust, and share them only intentionally. Anyone with access to a backup can read its contents. Reminder preferences need to be set again on a new device.

Notifications

Android practice reminders are scheduled locally and use device notification permissions. Their delivery depends on operating-system settings and restrictions. The PWA does not provide scheduled local reminders while closed.

This website and service providers

This landing website has no sign-in form, advertising scripts, analytics integration or application database. It does not set its own tracking cookies or store your learning data. Images and page assets are served with the website; no external font service is used.

GitHub Pages hosts the landing site, and GitHub Releases distributes APK files when available. Firebase Hosting serves the web app, while Firebase Authentication and Firestore provide the account and summary services. Providers may process IP addresses, request logs, browser or device information and other technical data under their own terms.

Read GitHub’s privacy statement and Firebase’s privacy and security information. Following a link to GitHub, Google or another service brings you under that service’s policies.

The reviewed IndoFreq source includes no advertising, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics integration, and no in-app research questionnaire or research consent collection. This does not mean hosting or authentication providers keep no operational logs.

Retention and removal

Local learning data remains until removed through device or browser storage controls, or replaced by an import where applicable. Signing out does not delete your local learning records or Firebase account. Uninstalling the app or clearing browser storage does not delete a cloud account or uploaded summary.

The app currently has no in-app account-deletion control and no published automatic retention schedule for cloud accounts and summaries. To request account or cloud-data removal, contact the maintainer through the public contact information on their GitHub profile. If no private contact method is listed, request one without posting your email, backup, password or other personal information publicly.

Questions and policy changes

Contact the maintainer through the same profile for privacy questions. This policy will be updated when the app’s data handling changes. The date above identifies this version.
