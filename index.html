Open Web App ↗

Look for “Install app” or “Add to Home Screen” in your browser’s menu. The wording depends on your device.

Your learning data

Local first.
Clear about the cloud.

Your detailed learning progress is primarily stored on your device. You can use guest mode, or sign in through Firebase Authentication.

Firestore stores an aggregate progress summary, not a full backup of your words and review history. Use the app’s export and import tools to keep a portable backup.
