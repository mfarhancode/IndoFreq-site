Indonesian vocabulary learning

Learn Indonesian,
one word at a time.

IndoFreq is a vocabulary app with flashcards, quizzes and spaced repetition. New words follow the frequency order in its vocabulary dataset, starting with more common words.

Android APK or browser. Guest mode available.

About the name

“Indo” for Indonesian.
“Freq” for frequency.

IndoFreq takes its name from the way its vocabulary is organised: by how frequently words are used, according to the supplied dataset.

A lower frequency rank means a word is more common within that data. Rank guides the order of new words; spaced repetition determines when studied words come back for review.

#

Frequency rank is an order, not a difficulty score.
It does not tell you how many times a word occurs, or how easy you will find it. Usage varies by context.

Using the app

Read the word.
Check what you remember.

Use flashcards for study, then a quiz for recall. Look up a word or keep it in your own list.

Flashcards & review

Read the Indonesian word, English meaning and usage examples. Choose “I know it” or “Still learning” to continue.

Vocabulary quizzes

Practise words due for review. If none are due, the quiz can use words already shown in flashcards.

Dictionary & word lists

Search Indonesian words and English meanings. Find words you have saved or marked known.

Daily goals & progress

Set a daily goal and see your learning activity and streak in the app.

Offline learning

Vocabulary and progress are stored locally. Open the PWA online first and let its offline files finish loading. Sign-in and summary uploads need a connection.

Export & import

Keep a JSON backup of your learning data and import it when moving to another installation.

Look up a word.

Search the dictionary, then open a word for its meaning, pronunciation and examples.

Privacy & data

Your progress stays
primarily on your device.

Learn as a guest, or use an account through Firebase Authentication. Your optional profile name is stored locally.

For signed-in users, Firestore stores an aggregate progress summary. It is not a full backup of saved words and review history. Use export and import for that.
